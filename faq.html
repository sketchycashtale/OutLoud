A few things
to know.

Start here for the short version of what OutLoud is, what happens in the group, and how to get in touch.

01What is OutLoud?

OutLoud is a community for expressing ideas, building confidence through practice, and sharing what the group does. That includes writing, thoughtful conversation, and showing up for one another.

02What is the Library?

The Library is an archive for community writing—articles, literature, reflections, and other pieces people want to share. Select a piece to open and read it. The current entries on this demo site are clearly marked as samples.

03What does 5Klick involve?

It is the group's morning running practice. Members share photographs and personal milestones, celebrating consistency and effort rather than comparing everyone's pace.

04What happens at a Court of Inquiry?

The group meets twice a week to explore a chosen topic. A session can include an icebreaker, a main speaker, open discussion, and a written report of the conclusion and remaining questions.

05Do I need to be a confident speaker already?

No. OutLoud is about practice, not performing perfection. You can listen, write, ask a question, or take a small turn when you are ready.

06Can I contribute an article or a field note?

Yes. Use ConnectWS to send a note about your idea. The writing on the public Library should be shared with the writer's permission; the examples there are placeholders, not actual member submissions.

07How can I join or suggest a change?

Open ConnectWS and tell us whether you are interested in joining or have a suggestion. The current form prepares an email draft; this static site does not send or store your submission.
