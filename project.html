Operation / 01 · Movement

THE MORNING MILE5Klick

5Klick is the group's everyday running practice: get out in the morning, move at your own pace, and share a little evidence of the effort. Photographs and personal milestones make the routine visible without turning it into a race.

What gets shared

01 / The moment

A photograph or short note from the morning.

02 / The milestone

A personal distance, consistency, or effort milestone.

Operation / 02 · Inquiry

COURT OF INQUIRYAsk better
questions.

COI is a twice-weekly group conversation built around one specific topic—from inflation to changes in education. The point is to bring different perspectives into the room, think carefully, and leave with a record of what was learned.

Each report captures

01 / Icebreaker

A prompt that gets everyone into the conversation.

02 / Main speaker

The person who frames the topic and starts the inquiry.

03 / The discussion

Key perspectives, questions, and points of disagreement.

04 / Conclusion

The group's takeaways and what remains open.
